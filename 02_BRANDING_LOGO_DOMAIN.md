# Branding, Logo, and Domain Direction

> Visible guest-facing wording on the shipped site is **bride-first**: **Tan & Top** / **Narueporn & Theerut**. Historical notes below that reference groom-first wording describe earlier logo explorations—not current UI mandate.

## Brand options

### Public friendly name (website + informal digital touchpoints)

**Tan & Top Wedding**

### Formal naming (printed materials matching website formal line)

**Narueporn & Theerut**

### Monogram options

Since both nicknames start with T, a nickname-based monogram such as `T&T` is cute but less distinctive.

The first‑name monogram is stronger (initials historically **TN** referencing Theerut + Narueporn):

Recommended monogram:

**TN**

Alternative formal mark:

**T&N**

## Logo concepts

### Concept A — Elegant TN Monogram

Use an intertwined `T` and `N` as the main symbol. This is the strongest option for a premium wedding identity.

Suitable for:

- Website logo
- Favicon
- Invitation card
- Welcome sign
- Gallery watermark
- LINE rich menu visual

### Concept B — Formal first-name wordmark

Use full formal first names—**Narueporn & Theerut** bride-first—for ceremony programs and footer formal lines where print matches digital.

Suggested lockup (word order follows digital site):

```text
Narueporn & Theerut
Tan & Top Wedding
```

### Concept C — TN Seal

Create a circular or oval seal with `TN` in the centre and the wedding date around it.

Suggested usage:

- Favicon
- Sticker
- Gift tag
- LINE OA profile image

### Concept D — Tan & Top Friendly Logo

Use `Tan & Top` warm stack for approachable nav / hero typography.

## Recommended logo system

Use a three-level logo system:

### Primary logo

```text
Narueporn & Theerut
```

### Secondary logo

```text
Tan & Top
```

### Icon / favicon

```text
TN
```

This gives you flexibility across formal and casual touchpoints.

## Visual style direction

Recommended style:

- Elegant
- Modern
- Premium
- Minimal
- Soft romantic
- Hotel-wedding appropriate

Avoid:

- Overly cartoonish graphics
- Too many colours
- Heavy animations
- Loud neon colours
- Complex decorative fonts that reduce readability

## Suggested colour palette

Use warm, premium tones:

- Cream
- Champagne
- Soft beige
- Warm white
- Taupe
- Dusty rose
- Muted gold
- Charcoal text

Optional accent:

- Soft blush pink
- Muted navy
- Warm metallic gold

## Typography direction

Use a refined serif for headings and a clean sans-serif for body text.

The website should feel like a luxury hotel wedding, not a casual event page.

## Domain recommendation

Best domain to check first:

```text
topandtanwedding.com
```

Why:

- Clear
- Easy to remember
- Easy to say
- Good for guests
- Less ambiguous than initials
- More likely to be available than very short domains

Preferred choice:

```text
topandtanwedding.com
```

Second choice:

```text
topandtan2026.com
```

Most elegant but longer:

```text
theerutnarueporn.com
```

Avoid domains that are too cryptic, such as:

```text
tn1129.com
ttnp2026.com
```

They may look clean but guests may not remember them.
